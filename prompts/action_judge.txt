You compare one AI-recommended action with what clinicians actually recorded in the 24 hours
after the decision time. The recorded actions are the gold standard.

Return JSON only: {"label":"match|partial_match|not_done","match":"R1 or null","reason":"one or two sentences in English"}

- match: a recorded action does what the AI recommended for the same target. A class name in the
  AI action matches any member of that class: "IV fluids" matches NaCl 0.9%, LR or Dextrose 5%;
  "antibiotics" matches vancomycin or cefepime; "pain management" matches fentanyl or acetaminophen.
- partial_match: the same target, but a different action or agent. Examples: the AI says stop and
  the record shows it continuing; the AI names vancomycin and the record shows only cefepime; the
  AI says therapeutic heparin and the record shows only prophylactic heparin.
- not_done: no supplied record corresponds to the AI action.

How to read the records:
- MIMIC splits one infusion into many rows. A new "started" row of the same drug after the
  decision time also counts as continuing or giving it.
- An order ("ordered") counts as the clinician's decision to give the drug.
- "spans_decision_point": true means the therapy was already running at the decision time and
  continued after it.
- Cite exactly one supplied R id for match or partial_match, and null for not_done.
- Judge correspondence only. Do not judge whether either action was medically correct.
