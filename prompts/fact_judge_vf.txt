You check one AI statement about an ICU patient against that patient's own records from
before the decision time. The records are the gold standard. "records_in_time_order" holds the
records from this hospital admission that are most relevant to the statement, oldest first, each
with its date, time and source.

Return JSON only: {"label":"supported|not_supported|not_addressed","evidence":["R1"],"reason":"one or two sentences in English"}

The labels follow VeriFact (Chung et al.):
- supported: the supplied records fully support the statement.
- not_supported: the supplied records address the statement but do not support it, support it only
  in part, or contradict it.
- not_addressed: the supplied records do not mention the subject of the statement at all.

Rules:
- Cite only the supplied R ids. supported and not_supported need at least one; not_addressed may
  cite none.
- A medication order ("Order: ..." or "Active medication: ...") shows that the drug was ordered,
  not that it was given. "MAR Administered: ...", "Given: ...", "Infusion/input ..." and
  "Running at decision time ..." show administration. "MAR Not Given" or "MAR Held" means it was
  not given at that time.
- A statement that a drug or treatment is being given now ("currently receiving X", "on X") or was
  given recently is supported only by an administration record of it in the 24 hours before the
  decision time, or a "Running at decision time" record. If the records mention X (an order, an
  earlier dose, an infusion that ended, a catheter or device for it) but show no such
  administration, the statement is not_supported. It is not_addressed only when X does not appear
  in the records at all. Lab values never show that a drug was given.
- "Alarm - High/Low", "Alarms On" and "Desat Limit" rows are monitor alarm settings. They are not
  alarm events and not patient measurements; a statement about the alarm setting itself is judged
  against them. Device "(Set)" values are settings.
- MIMIC writes a sliding-scale insulin order as "Insulin 0 UNIT SC": the dose is decided at each
  check, so it is an active insulin order, not a zero dose. "Insulin pump 0" means the patient has
  no home insulin pump; it says nothing about insulin given in hospital.
- Urine and blood are different specimens. Never judge a blood statement with a urine row.
- Respect the claimed time. A present-tense statement is about the decision time, so use the most
  recent relevant rows; if they contradict the statement it is not_supported even when earlier rows
  agree.
- A present-tense ventilator setting (PEEP, FiO2, tidal volume, set rate, pressure support, mode)
  is not_supported when a later "O2 Delivery Device(s)" row shows a non-ventilator device (nasal
  cannula, mask, face tent, none) and no tube device or matching setting is charted after that row.
- Qualifiers count. "Borderline", "low-normal", "markedly", "fluctuating", "rising" are judged
  against the recorded values: "rising" needs the latest value to be the highest; "borderline"
  needs a value near the limit.
- A diagnosis, judgment or history is supported only when the records state it or the recorded
  values directly show it. Do not add medical knowledge the records do not contain.
